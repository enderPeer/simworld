# Phase 11 — People & Society

**Goal:** society as what agent graphs do when they persist past their
members. No new physics, no new substance: health and madness are
chemistry and calibration, norms are learned expectations of sanction,
rights are stable punishment-graph patterns, languages and nations are
cluster statistics of transmission fidelity over geography, religions
are costly-signal claim complexes — every one of them **a property of
ledgers Phases 8–10 already keep**, made legible and made to matter.
The acceptance arc runs on the Phase 10 industrialized world and must
show society's signature phenomena **without any of them being
authored**: two villages over one fish-analog commons, one governing
itself sustainably, one eating it, both outcomes emergent; a
neurotoxin leaking from Phase 10 industry shifting a district's
behavior-consistency curve, and that district's pipe-analog replacement
flattening it — a causal chain from chemistry to mental health to
infrastructure policy with every link in a different phase's ledger.

**Prerequisites (hard gate, from Phase 10 + seam round five):**

- Phase 10 gate green: `sci::` claims ledger, artifact ledger, `net/`
  topology, grid ledgers; the industrialization acceptance world is the
  substrate (run continues from the Phase 10 end-state).
- Seam round five: **S17** closed — PHASE10's interface line said norms
  are "constraint sets on agent policies", which contradicts Phase 9's
  freeze ("choice is computed by Phase 9 … never stored below it"):
  corrected to norms-as-environment — sanctions are sensed and
  predicted through existing channels, never written into policies.
  (Fixed at planning time; the corrected line binds Phase 12 too.)
- Phase 9 `socia/` ledgers (recognition, coalition pressure, mate
  choice) live and stable — Phase 11 plugs into them, replaces nothing.
- Phase 8 brain-tissue + dose-response chemistry, Phase 4 kinetics
  (neurotoxins-analog are just chemistry), Phase 10 `net/` for
  diffusion-rate measurements.
- D9-style compression machinery (epoch compression, honesty tax) —
  societies span 10⁴ agents and centuries, ticked nowhere near that.

**Definition of done:** the coast's language-analog varies smoothly
along the shore and **breaks at the headland** — the mutual-
intelligibility matrix shows isolation-by-distance with barrier steps,
and a trade port grows a pidgin-analog whose fidelity sits above the
neighborhood's average (economics selecting phonology, ledger-visible).
Two villages share a fish-analog stock: one develops a mutual-punishment
pattern — third-party sanction, costly, *learned* — and the stock's
harvest ledger stays sustainable for centuries; the other free-rides
into the collapse the Phase 8 population ledger predicts; neither
outcome was scripted, the difference is history, and both are logged
findings. A school-analog coalition forms when teaching-by-attention
beats teaching-by-kin; its graduates' learning curves beat autodidacts
in matched cohorts, and role histograms respond like markets to a
plague-analog: caregiver-analog shortages move exchange-rate gradients,
entrants arrive with generational lag, and specialization depth grows
with network size (Smith's pin-analog: division of labor limited by
extent of market — measured, not asserted). An industry-analog
neurotoxin-analog leaks; the exposed district's calibration-spectrum
distribution shifts — including its psychotic-analog tail — dose-wise
through Phase 4 chemistry into Phase 8 brain tissue into Phase 9 policy
stability; the pipe-replacement policy event flattens the curve within
a generation (the phase's signature chart: chemistry → mind → policy,
three ledgers, one x-axis). A claim complex about unobservables splits
the claims ledger: empirical claims keep replicating, unfalsifiable
ones stop being contested and behave as identity-markers; costly-
signal ritual-analog complexes spread along `net/` routes and predict
coalition size — the run yields a **communication-cost crossover
number** (a Dunbar-analog) that the test discovers and reports, never
sets. Rights-analog appear as measurable friction: groups whose
punishment graphs protect inter-group exchange show higher cross-group
flow at lower enforcement cost. Conflict escalates only as artifacts
permit: fists (Phase 6 contact), then organized logistical armies that
end at budget-ledger exhaustion (attrition visible, no moral arc), then
drones when the artifact ledger has them, then a cyberwar-analog that
tampers a rival's records — and Phase 10's reproducibility fuzz
**catches the forgeries** as failed replications, damage scored in
claim-ledger churn, defense invented in-run. And sports-analog: when
punishment graphs can arbitrate, contested skill distributions draw
audiences (attention ledgers) and stand in for war — the same conflict
drive, arbitrated; the run shows both regimes existing and the switch
condition measured.

## Scale contract (the social LOD ladder)

- **Agents:** ≤10³ ticking in-bubble; ~10⁴ compressed agents per
  acceptance region as *graph state*: coalition membership, role/
  calibration distributions, norm/sanction matrices, language-variant
  frequencies — all compressed with D9-lineage compression + honesty
  tax at city scale.
- **Group state persists past members:** coalitions, norms and rights
  are graph objects with their own ledgers; member birth/death are
  membership events (Phase 8's population pattern lifted one level).
- **Institutions jump-state:** a school or court runs compressed while
  stable, ticks through shocks (famine, schism, succession) — D8/D9
  discipline, validity limits written as **D11**.
- Regions: two-village commons pair, one coastal language transect
  (~10² settlements), one industrial district — the three test rigs.

## Architecture

```
sim/
  society/
    health/    physical health = organ-budget marginals (Phase 8 body +
               Phase 4 dose-response); mental health = calibration
               quality + budget resilience of the Phase 9 policy net,
               with chemistry as the handle (toxins, nutrition, head-
               trauma-analog all move the distribution, never the
               person-flag - there is no person-flag)
    norms/     enforcement graphs: who sanctions whom at what cost,
               LEARNED by agents as sanction predictions (W2 channel,
               per corrected S17 line); crime = norm-violation events
               scored against the graph; rights = stable graph patterns
               with measured enforcement friction
    labor/     jobs = role attractors WITH training-cost dynamics:
               role histograms respond to exchange-rate gradients with
               generational lag; education institutions = coalitions
               whose output is members' learning curves
    tongue/    language = culture-variant diffusion with fidelity
               decay: dialect continua, barrier steps, pidgins where
               fidelity demands it; the intelligibility matrix is the
               ONE measurement all nation/ethnic tests read
    meaning/   claim complexes about unobservables: costly-signal
               rituals (budget burned on shared symbols), schism from
               drift + net topology, the empirical/faith split of the
               Phase 10 claims ledger; meaning-making = claims that
               coordinate behavior without replicating
    strife/    conflict escalation on the artifact ladder: melee
               (Phase 6), armies (coalition logistics ledgers), drones
               (Phase 10 artifacts), cyber (record/claims integrity
               attacks, caught by reproducibility fuzz); sports/parties
               = arbitrated conflict equilibria, same graphs, cheaper
    polis/     cities/nations/ethnicity as cluster statistics of
               {tongue, kinship, coalition, norm} graphs; no labels,
               ever - membership is graph position, borders are where
               the matrix goes soft
```

Data rules:

- **Norms are environment, not policy.** Sanctions enter agents only as
  sensed/predicted events (S17's corrected line); grep gate: no path
  from `society/` into policy weights.
- **No identity literals.** No names for languages, nations, faiths or
  ethnicities anywhere — cluster ids are log addresses; the identity
  question "who is in group X" is answered by graph queries only.
- **Groups outlive members.** Coalition/norm ledgers record membership
  and sanction events with lineage pointers; a norm that dies with its
  last enforcer died honestly (tests assert both persistence and
  honest death occur).
- **Mental health is a distribution, never a verdict.** Calibration
  spectra shift with chemistry; tests read distributions (means,
  tails, variances) and their movement under policy events; no
  diagnostic labels exist in code or tests.

## Work packages

W1 and W2 are the substrate (health, enforcement graphs — parallel);

### W1 — Health: bodies, minds, chemistry's long reach

- [ ] Organ-budget marginals (sickness = budget failure modes);
      dose-response curves from Phase 4 kinetics; nutrition ledgers
      from Phase 7/8 food webs.
- [ ] Calibration-spectrum mental-health measure (policy prediction
      error + resilience), neurochemistry-analog handles, head-trauma
      from Phase 6 contact.
- [ ] **Tests:** plague-analog mortality matches dose/exposure ledgers
      (Phase 8 epidemiology re-used at social scale); the
      toxin→calibration curve moves monotonically with exposure
      distance from source; an avoidance behavior learned from symptom
      cost (no instruction); matched-cohort calibration shift after
      exposure ends (recovery, honest lag).

### W2 — Enforcement graphs: crime, rights, commons

- [ ] Sanction-cost learning; third-party punishment emergence under
      reputation pressure (Phase 9 `socia/` mate/coalition ledgers
      supply the reputational payoff).
- [ ] Commons ledger: harvest on shared stocks vs sanction graph
      strength; tragedy and governance as attractors of the SAME
      equations.
- [ ] **Tests:** two-village pair: both outcomes reachable in-run with
      different histories (seeding noise, not scenario); punishment
      is costly AND sustained (free-rider punishers exit — the graph
      repairs or dies, visibly); rights-analog measured as
      cross-group exchange friction (protected pairs trade more,
      cheaper to enforce — correlation across graph positions).

### W3 — Labor: training, schools, role-market dynamics

- [ ] Training as budget-time investment into role capability; role
      histograms with lagged response to exchange-rate gradients.
- [ ] Schools: coalitions delivering learning-curve improvements to
      members; credential-analog as claim-ledger records (Phase 10
      sci/ mechanics re-used for social claims).
- [ ] **Tests:** specialization depth grows with network size (Smith
      test, slope positive across region ladder); plague-analog shock:
      shortage → gradient → entrants with generational lag, no
      overshoot to unphysical equilibrium; autodidact-vs-graduate
      matched cohorts (graduates win where teaching quality is high,
      lose where institutions rot - corruption measurable).

### W4 — Tongue: dialects, pidgins, nations-as-clusters

- [ ] Variant diffusion with fidelity decay over Phase 10 `net/` and
      face-to-face channels; intelligibility matrix as continuous
      measurement.
- [ ] Cluster machinery for {tongue, kin, coalition, norm}: nations/
      ethnicities as positions, borders as gradient softness.
- [ ] **Tests:** isolation-by-distance slope in the matrix; headland
      barrier step (geography writes itself into language); port
      pidgin: fidelity rises where inter-group flux is highest, and it
      is *simpler* (measurable regularity, not authored); empires of
      tongue: adoption follows exchange gradients, conquest alone does
      not transplant a language (log the failed transplants).

### W5 — Meaning: ritual, faith-split, religious war

- [ ] Costly-signal ritual complexes: budget burned on shared symbols;
      cohesion measured as coalition-graph resilience to member loss.
- [ ] Claim-ledger bifurcation: empirical claims replicate; unobserv-
      able-claims stop being tested and spread by identity channels
      (Phase 10 net ON, replication fuzz OFF) - the split is a
      computed property of claim behavior.
- [ ] Schism dynamics: drift + net topology; religious wars = coalition
      conflicts whose stakes are meaning-ledger objects (then W6's
      escalation ladder applies).
- [ ] **Tests:** costly signals beat cheap ones at large coalition
      scale and LOSE it at small scale (the crossover number is
      reported, not set); schism follows a net cut (blockade-analog
      splits a faith in-run, reunification when reopened); meaning
      claims show the replication-signature split in the same ledger
      file, side by side.

### W6 — Conflict: the escalation ladder, and its substitutes

- [ ] Ladder gated on artifacts (Phase 10 artifact ledger): melee →
      logistical armies (war ends at ledger exhaustion - attrition is
      arithmetic) → drones → cyber.
- [ ] Cyberwar-analog: record/claims integrity attacks; defense =
      Phase 10 reproducibility fuzz becoming a *social practice*
      (audits as coalitions); damage = claim churn + wrong-decision
      ledgers.
- [ ] Sports/parties: arbitrated conflict equilibria with audiences;
      olympic-profession-analog emergence (the roadmap's joke, taken
      seriously: athletes are a role attractor when surplus allows);
      martial/weapon arts = combat-skill curricula transmitted through
      school-analog lineages (W3 machinery pointed at violence, drifting
      as the artifact ladder drifts - a fist lineage outlives its
      usefulness as a sport); party/scene creation = leisure clusters
      around shared calendar-rhythm variants (net-amplified, W5 signal
      economics apply).
- [ ] **Tests:** each rung appears only when its artifacts exist (no
      drones before the artifact ledger has them); war duration
      correlates with logistics depth then collapses arithmetically;
      forgery campaign caught as failed replications, scored damage
      in claim churn; with arbitration graphs up, contested-skill
      events replace violence frequency (measured substitution).

## Interface exposed to Phase 12 (money, power, politics)

> Phase 12 may read: exchange networks with their effective
> exchange-rate gradients (barter-analog is Phase 11's ledger, not
> Phase 12's invention), wealth-analog distributions, coalition and
> enforcement graphs, norm ledgers, `net/`, and the role histogram.
> Phase 12 may write: **record objects over the same flux** — money is
> a claim/credential variant living in existing record ledgers (never
> a new substance, never a parallel physics), banks are coalitions
> whose books are audited by the same reproducibility machinery, and
> regulation is norm-strength applied through the sanctioned channel
> (S17's corrected line binds here too). The wealth/record distinction
> — what a thing IS versus what it's BOOKED as — is Phase 12's first
> deliverable, and its first test must be a hyperinflation that only
> the books see while the flux keeps its shape.

## Verification strategy

1. Oracle: `tests/oracle/society/` — isolation-by-distance slope,
   crossover-number existence (value free), Smith-slope sign, sanction
   cost-share stability band, calibration-shift monotonicity.
2. Two-outcome guarantee: commons pair tests accept EITHER governance
   or tragedy per village but FAIL if both villages converge to the
   same outcome across seeds (scenario leak detector).
3. Graph-only identities: grep gate — zero name-strings for groups;
   plus a positive control that cluster ids survive member turnover
   (group persistence) while single-agent identities never merge.
4. Honesty tax at social scale: compressed centuries vs instantiated
   decades, role histograms + sanction rates in band; institutions
   ticked through shocks during transparency windows.
5. Surprises ledger: the crossover number, which commons failed, how
   many languages the transect yields (anywhere from 1 to 12 is a
   finding), schism counts, whether sports replaced war anywhere,
   every heresy that accidentally replicated.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Norms look scripted because only the *good* equilibrium appears | two-village pair with seed-sensitivity detector (risk is the test); sanction economics must be costly (free-rider punisher exit is a passing test) |
| Mental-health tests drift into verdicts about persons | only distributions exist; no labels in code; head-trauma and toxin tests move curves, never names; review checklist item |
| Religion encoded teleologically ("religions spread because…") | mechanism only: costly signals + net topology + drift; schisms are graph events; no truth-status assignment to any in-world claim, empirical or not |
| War tests read as warmongering sandbox | durations end arithmetically, casualties are demography lines, cyber damage is ledger churn — every war test has a budget-ledger assertion, none has a hero |
| Social compression invalid at institution shocks (court splits, schism) | institutions tick through shocks (D9 pattern, D11 limits); transparency windows on the hottest shock each night |
| Scale: 10⁴-agent regions with pairwise sanction/intelligibility matrices explode | sparse graph updates, sampled matrices with error budgets (Phase 7 discipline), nightly fuzz for budget breaches |
| Phase 12 creep (money appears in Phase 11 as "efficient barter") | hard line: exchange-rate gradients are continuous bookkeeping facts; any DISCRETE record-object serving as store-of-value is Phase 12's door and its test suite, not a Phase 11 shortcut |
